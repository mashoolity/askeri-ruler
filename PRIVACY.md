# Askeri Ruler — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

Askeri Ruler is an on-screen guide-line tool that runs entirely on your computer.

**What the app collects:** nothing. Askeri Ruler does not collect, store, transmit or share any
personal information, usage data, screen contents or images. It makes no network connections.

**What the app stores locally:** your preferences (hotkey, line color and sizes, rotation speed)
in a settings file on your own computer (`%APPDATA%\AskeriRuller\settings.ini`). Uninstalling the
app or deleting that file removes them.

**Keyboard and mouse input:** while a guide line is on screen, the app watches for the Esc key so
it can remove the lines, and reads mouse movement to position and rotate the line. This input is
used only for that purpose, in memory, and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to the publisher via the contact details on the
app's Microsoft Store page.
